## Why PDF Menus and Delivery Apps Quietly Drain Margin

When a diner scans a table QR code in Istanbul, Riyadh, or Cairo, two things usually happen:

1. A heavy 15 MB PDF opens, forcing the guest to pinch-and-zoom on slow mobile data with zero language switching.
2. Or the restaurant pushes diners toward aggregator apps that charge **20% to 30% commission** while owning the customer relationship.

A custom web-based QR platform fixes both problems at once: **instant page loads under 1 second**, native Google search visibility (`Schema.org Menu` + `Restaurant`), and **0% commission** on direct orders.

### How Zero-Commission Ordering Works

Instead of forcing tourists to register an account or pay aggregator fees:

1. The customer browses the menu in their native language and sees prices converted live in their preferred currency.
2. They add items to a lightweight cart right in the browser.
3. Tapping **Order via WhatsApp** generates a pre-formatted message in the restaurant's operational language with item names, quantities, and total price.

```ts
type CartItem = { name: string; qty: number; priceTry: number };

export function buildWhatsAppOrderUrl(phone: string, items: CartItem[], table?: string) {
  const lines = items.map((i) => `• ${i.qty}x ${i.name}, ${i.qty * i.priceTry} TRY`);
  const total = items.reduce((sum, i) => sum + i.qty * i.priceTry, 0);
  const header = table ? `Table #${table} Order:` : "New Direct Order:";
  const text = [header, ...lines, `Total: ${total} TRY`].join("\n");
  return `https://wa.me/${phone}?text=${encodeURIComponent(text)}`;
}
```
